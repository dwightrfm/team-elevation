Hot States

A hot state does not stop you writing business there. It governs whether the points count on the SMD run. If business is written in a hot state, the writing agent or whoever overrides it has to be licensed there, and in two states appointed as well.

This is a snapshot, not live. Licensing data pulled from the mywfg Licenses & Appointments report on 2026-07-13, three weeks old. The canonical source is the Team Licenses Google Sheet, and when it disagrees with this page, the sheet wins. Re-pull before September 30.
