Rule 01

Two a day. Same core. Forever.

Not more names. More trips around the same names. Templeton's entire system is a fixed core database you never graduate from. The Builders Playbook says it in four words: many people, many times.

Two a day is small on purpose. A system you can run on your worst day is the only kind that compounds.

Rule 02

Eighty percent goes to proven senders

Past behavior is the only predictor. A person who has sent you one name is worth more than fifty people you think should. Everyone else is a hope, not a source. That is why List 1 is ranked by the Got column and not by how much you like someone.

Rule 03

Never ask twice

One ask per relationship, ever. The second ask is what turns a friend into someone who lets your call ring out. You have a finite amount of that currency and you have been spending it at full speed.

If you already asked and got nothing, it is not the relationship and it is not the script. It is the stage.

Rule 04

"I don't know anybody" has exactly two causes

  1. They are at Visibility. Stop asking. Go give.
  2. They are at Credibility with no language. They trust you and still cannot pattern-match a prospect. Give them a story, never a category.

Either way: do not ask again. Go find a referral for them instead.

Rule 05

Close every loop within 48 hours

When a referral comes in, report back to whoever sent it. What happened, what you did, thank you. Templeton and Randall both put this at number one for why referral streams die: the referrer never hears the outcome, so they never send another. Cheapest thing on this page and the most skipped.

Rule 06

Prospects exit. Sources never do.

Your current follow-up engine ranks everyone by days overdue, which means the people ignoring you rise to the top precisely because they are ignoring you. Your worst leads get your best attention every morning and the list only grows.

  • Prospects: 7 touches over 45 days, then out of the daily list into a dormant pool that resurfaces twice a year.
  • Sources: never exit. 18 to 20 planned touches a year. Zero asks.

Seed stories, the trigger install

This is what fixes referrals that go nowhere. Kevin Gainey, Miriam Catron and Alex Lewis all referred you and none converted, because they cannot recognize a prospect. Categories do not install a pattern. Stories do. Say one, then stop talking.

Seed 1 / overtime with nothing to show
We just sat down with a guy who'd been pulling doubles for two years and still had nothing in savings. He wasn't broke, he was just unprotected. Watching it click for him was the best part of my month.

Do not follow this with "know anybody like that?" That turns a seed into an ask and kills it.

Seed 2 / newly commission-only
Worked with a couple where the husband just went commission-only. Great earner, zero floor under him. We built the floor. She said it was the first time she'd slept through the night in months.
Seed 3 / new baby, no plan
Had a couple reach out right after their first kid. Smart people, good jobs, and no plan at all if something happened to either of them. Ninety minutes and it was handled.

Collect, access, give, close

Collect an offered introduction

They already offered. You are not asking, you are closing a loop they opened.

[Name], you mentioned a while back you'd connect me with [person]. Life clearly got busy on both ends. Is that still someone you think would want the conversation, or has that changed?
Ask for the intro, never the number

A number is a cold call with extra steps. An introduction is a warm appointment.

Rather than me reaching out cold, would you text the three of us so it comes from you?
Room access

Ask to attend, not to present. Never pitch the first time you are in someone's room.

I'd love to just come sit in on one of your [events / meetings] as a guest. Not to speak, not to pitch, I just want to see what you've built. When's the next one?
Giving one
